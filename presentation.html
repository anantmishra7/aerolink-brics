Spatial Blindspots

Hyper-Local Omission

10–20 fixed government stations per megacity create massive 50 km² blindspots. Stubble fires, nocturnal brick kilns, unpaved industrial transit, and fugitive stack bypasses evade municipal sensors completely.

Vector Invisibility

Transboundary Transport

Particulate matter (PM2.5) and gaseous precursors (NO2, SO2) do not respect jurisdictional borders. Seasonal winds carry toxic plumes 300+ km under thermal inversion lids, blind-siding downwind megacities.

Geopolitical Friction

Data Siloing Across Borders

Sovereign nations and municipal agencies refuse to upload raw domestic telemetry or industrial emissions to centralized foreign servers, paralyzing coordinated multinational mitigation.
